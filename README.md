I build **reproducible geospatial research software** that turns official records, satellite observations and field data into traceable evidence. My interests span urban analysis, protected areas, climate adaptation and spatial AI. I make the **sources, uncertainty and limits** visible rather than treating model outputs as facts.

## Research approach

**Place → Evidence → Interpretation → Decision.** I distinguish observed, derived and modelled results, document methodological limits, and treat insufficient evidence as a valid outcome. These projects have different maturity levels; an archive or working application is not, by itself, scientific validation.
